Questions

What are the fees?
They depend on the course and batch, so they aren’t published here. Send a WhatsApp and you’ll get the current figure.
O Level 7707 or IGCSE 0452?
They cover the same seven topics and are examined with the same two papers. Your school decides which one you are entered for.
Do I need O Level Accounting for A Level?
It helps, because AS starts with double entry and financial statements and moves quickly. Students starting fresh at AS can catch up, but should expect to work hardest in the first term.
Are there online classes?
Ask on WhatsApp for online options. Lessons are also available on his YouTube channel.
Is my progress on this site saved?
The syllabus tracker, score log and trainer score are saved in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
