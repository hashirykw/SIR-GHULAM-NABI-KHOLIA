About

Sir Ghulam Nabi Kholia

Sir Ghulam Nabi Kholia teaches Cambridge Accounting in Karachi for O Level, IGCSE and AS & A Level students, as part of the commerce faculty.

He has taught O and A Level Accounts at Hexis College, whose poster for his classes carried the line “accounts is the language of business”. Alongside his classes he publishes accounting lessons on YouTube.

This site is built on the same idea. Once a student can say why a transaction is a debit to one account and a credit to another, the formats that follow, from ledgers to company statements, stop being things to memorise.
