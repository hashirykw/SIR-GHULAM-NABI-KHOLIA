Where accounting marks go missing.

Most lost marks in Paper 2 aren’t about hard topics. They’re about presentation the examiner can’t give credit for. Fix these habits first.

01

Headings left off

An income statement needs the business name, the statement’s title and “for the year ended”. Each can carry a mark.

Fix: write the full heading before any figure.
02

Workings hidden

A wrong total with no working scores nothing. The same total with workings shown can still earn the method marks.

Fix: label every working and refer to it.
03

Ledger accounts not balanced off

Balance c/d and b/d, with the date and both totals ruled off, is part of the answer, not decoration.

Fix: close every account the same way, every time.
04

Evaluation without a decision

“Advise” questions want both sides, then a recommendation backed by a figure from the question.

Fix: argue for, argue against, then decide.
